# CPCB National Air Quality Index (India) & Winter GRAP Advisories
Source: Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB), Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC), India.

## AQI Categories & PM2.5 (24-hr µg/m³)
- **Good (0–50)**: PM2.5 0–30 µg/m³. Minimal health impact. Normal outdoor exercise and full cross-ventilation safe.
- **Satisfactory (51–100)**: PM2.5 31–60 µg/m³. Minor breathing discomfort to sensitive people.
- **Moderate (101–200)**: PM2.5 61–90 µg/m³. Breathing discomfort to people with asthma, lungs, and heart disease.
- **Poor (201–300)**: PM2.5 91–120 µg/m³. Breathing discomfort to most people on prolonged exposure. Avoid roadside jogging; wear N95 outdoors.
- **Very Poor (301–400)**: PM2.5 121–250 µg/m³. Respiratory illness on prolonged exposure. Keep children and elders indoors; seal drafts; run mechanical HEPA filtration.
- **Severe (401–500)**: PM2.5 >250 µg/m³. Affects healthy people and seriously impacts those with existing diseases. Trigger Smog Emergency Mode: halt outdoor exertion, avoid burning biomass/waste, shift to public transit or WFH.

## Winter GRAP (Graded Response Action Plan) Key Citizen Actions
- Stage II–IV (AQI > 300): Avoid open burning of leaves, garbage, or wood/coal sigris; carpool or take electric metro/bus; keep engines switched off at red lights; avoid dusty home sweeping and use wet mopping.
- Winter thermal inversions trap surface exhaust between 20:00 and 09:00 due to low planetary boundary layer / mixing height (<300 m). Midday solar warming (12:30–15:30) typically lifts mixing height and creates the lowest outdoor PM2.5 window.
