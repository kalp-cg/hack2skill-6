# WHO 2021 Global Air Quality Guidelines & Exposure Equivalence
Source: World Health Organization (WHO) Global Air Quality Guidelines (2021) & Berkeley Earth PM2.5 Equivalence Analysis.

## WHO 2021 Short-Term Guidelines (24-hour / 8-hour)
- **PM2.5 (24-hr)**: 15 µg/m³ (Annual: 5 µg/m³). Fine particles ≤2.5 µm penetrate deep into lung alveoli and enter systemic circulation.
- **PM10 (24-hr)**: 45 µg/m³ (Annual: 15 µg/m³). Coarse dust, road resuspension, and construction debris.
- **NO2 (24-hr)**: 25 µg/m³. Emitted by petrol/diesel vehicles and unvented indoor LPG/gas cooking burners; triggers asthma exacerbations.
- **O3 (Peak 8-hr)**: 100 µg/m³. Photochemical pollutant irritating airways.

## Cigarette Equivalence Approximation
- Berkeley Earth epidemiologic rule of thumb: breathing an average PM2.5 concentration of **22 µg/m³ over 24 hours** (approx. 330 µg inhaled at 15 m³/day breathing volume) carries a rough statistical mortality risk equivalent to **1 cigarette per day**.
- Always communicate this explicitly as an **approximation** to help families visualize exposure reduction intuitively—not a medical diagnosis.
