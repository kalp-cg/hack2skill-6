# MoHFW & ICMR Health Advisories for Vulnerable Groups During Severe Smog
Source: Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW) & Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR) Air Pollution Health Guidance.

## Children & Infants
- Children breathe faster per kg body weight and walk closer to vehicle tailpipe height.
- When AQI > 200: cancel vigorous outdoor sports, shift school commutes away from arterial roads, and keep classroom/bedroom windows closed during morning rush (06:30–09:30).
- When AQI > 300 (Severe): strictly prohibit outdoor running, cycling, or playground exertion for children, especially those with asthma. Keep rescue inhalers accessible.

## Elders, Asthma, COPD, Heart Conditions & Pregnancy
- High PM2.5 increases acute airway inflammation, wheezing, blood pressure spikes, and adverse pregnancy outcomes.
- Never advise stopping prescribed inhalers or medications.
- **Emergency Red-Flag Escalation**: If any household member experiences persistent wheezing, shortness of breath at rest, chest pain/tightness, bluish lips, or dizziness, advise seeking immediate medical care or visiting the nearest doctor/hospital. - Pets (dogs/cats): shorten winter smog walks to 5–10 minutes during midday hours and wipe paws with a damp cloth on return.
