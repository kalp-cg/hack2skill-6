# Indoor Air Quality Hacks, Masks, Purifier CADR & Myth-Busting
Source: US EPA Indoor Air Guide, ASHRAE Filtration Guidance, and IIT-Delhi / Urban IAQ Field Studies.

## Purifier Sizing (CADR Formula)
- Target **4.8–5 Air Changes per Hour (ACH)** during severe smog.
- For an 8–9 ft ceiling room: `Required CADR (m³/h) ≈ Room Area (sq ft) × 1.35`.
  - Example: 120 sq ft bedroom → ~165 m³/h CADR; 200 sq ft living room → ~270 m³/h CADR.
- Use True HEPA (H13) mechanical filtration only. Avoid ionizers or ozone-emitting purifiers which create lung-irritating ozone.
- Place purifier at least 30 cm away from walls/curtains in the occupied breathing zone; close doors and windows while running.

## Low-Cost & Zero-Cost Indoor Hacks
- **Timed Ventilation**: Open opposite windows for 10–15 minutes only during the lowest outdoor AQI window (usually 13:00–15:30) to clear indoor CO2. Seal windows before 17:00.
- **Window & Door Sealing**: Press damp rolled towels or foam weatherstripping along balcony sliding doors and under-door gaps during AQI > 300 episodes (cuts infiltration ~25–30%).
- **Wet Mopping**: Never dry-sweep during smog days; damp microfiber mopping prevents settled soot resuspension.
- **Kitchen Protocol**: High-heat tadka/frying and LPG gas flames spike indoor PM2.5 past 250 µg/m³ and NO2 past 100 µg/m³. Run the kitchen chimney/exhaust fan 5 min before cooking, cover pans, avoid deep-frying during peak evening inversion, and shift boiling to an electric kettle or induction stove.
- **Zero Indoor Combustion**: Pause agarbatti (incense), dhoop, scented candles, and mosquito coils on high-AQI days.
- **Indoor Plants Myth-Busting**: Potted plants (snake plant, areca palm) improve mood and humidity slightly, **but they cannot meaningfully clean PM2.5 smog** in a real room—peer-reviewed studies show you would need 100+ plants per square meter to match one HEPA purifier. Always rely on sealing + mechanical HEPA/MERV-13 filtration.
- **N95 / N99 Masks**: Surgical cloth masks leak >60% of PM2.5. Use a tight-fitting N95/KF94/N99 respirator (pinch nose bridge; no beard gap) whenever outdoor AQI exceeds 150.
